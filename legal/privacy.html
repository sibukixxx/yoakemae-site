Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Drink & Dream ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

Information We Collect

The App collects the following information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Drink logs (type, quantity, time, optional reason/memo)
  • Sleep quality logs (difficulty falling asleep, night awakening, etc.)
  • Morning condition logs (hardness, sleepiness, mood, etc.)
  • Dream diary entries (optional personal notes)
  • App preferences (country, subscription status)

We do NOT collect:

  • Your name or email address (anonymous use)
  • Location data
  • Photos or contacts
  • Health data from HealthKit

How We Store Your Data

All your personal data is stored locally on your device only.

  • Data is saved using SwiftData on your iPhone
  • No personal data is transmitted to external servers
  • Anonymous crash reports may be sent to improve app stability (see Third-Party Services)
  • No cloud synchronization in the current version
  • You can export your data at any time in CSV or JSON format (Pro feature)
  • Deleting the app will permanently remove all data

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

Apple App Store

In-app purchases and subscriptions

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.)

Anonymous crash reporting to improve app stability. No personal data or health records are included. Disabled in debug builds.

Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for use by children under 17 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
